# Finding about Bloons

## `fast`

**Meaning:** Bloon speed.

The variable `fast` is used as the speed variable for Bloons. This naming was confirmed by the game's developer, Ramaf.

- **Variable:** `fast`
- **Meaning:** Speed
- **Status:** Confirmed by developer statement.
