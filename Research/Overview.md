## Implementation reference

The separate game-code repository used for implementation research is [BTDX-source](https://github.com/alert-bake/BTDX-source). Keep references to it in research documentation where relevant; it does not need to appear in the public-facing README.

## Evidence policy

- **Source-confirmed** means the inspected implementation contains the behaviour or value; it does not prove every build or mode uses it.
- **Gameplay-observed** means the behaviour was directly observed in-game. Record platform, version, and setup.
- **Unverified report** means a claim is recorded for investigation but has not been independently reproduced or confirmed in the inspected source.
- **Unresolved** means evidence is incomplete, contradictory, or does not identify the exact implementation.

Do not silently merge platform differences or resolve disagreements without evidence. Do not import BTD5 or BTD6 values to fill gaps in BTDX documentation.
