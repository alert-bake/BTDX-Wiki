# MOAB-class Bloons

MOAB-class Bloons are large blimp enemies. The BTDX community reference defines the category as Bloons that require more than 74 hits to pop their outer layer. They are described as resistant to most status effects, although some tower upgrades deal bonus damage to MOAB layers.
