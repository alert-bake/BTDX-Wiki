<badge>Research draft · MOAB-class</badge>

## Overview

Nightmare-only and described as the strongest non-Bounty Bloon. 15,000 hits to pop; moves at 56.25% Red speed. Releases 15 Rainbows and three Prismatics off-track every two seconds, summons three Bricks every ten seconds, and drops 18 Rocket Blimps plus one Z.O.M.G.
