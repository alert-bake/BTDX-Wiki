# M.B.R.C. (Mega Big Rubbery Carrier)

<badge>Research draft · MOAB-class</badge>

## Overview

Nightmare-only. 2,000 hits to pop; moves at 37.5% of Red-Bloon speed. It launches 15 Purples and three Prismatics off-track every two seconds, summons three Bricks on-track every ten seconds, and drops 32 Bricks plus one B.R.C. when popped.
