# Ninja Bloon

<badge>Research draft</badge>

## Overview

Ninja Bloons have Camo and fire homing ninja stars that stun towers every 1.5–4.5 seconds. When popped, they release a Splitting Camo Bloon of the same colour. Higher-colour variants are faster, have longer range, and stun for longer.

## Classification

- **Category:** Special Bloon / special form
- **Platform/version checked:** None; community source only.
- **HP / hits:** Red 9 HP; Blue 18; Green 27; Yellow 36; Pink 45; Rainbow 90 (reported)
- **Children / result:** Splitting Camo Bloon of matching colour
