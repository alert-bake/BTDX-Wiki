# Demon Ball

<badge>Research draft · Bounty hazard</badge>

## Overview

A Clown Bounty hazard that replaces Star Balls in the Rubber Terror stage. The source reports 80 HP initially and says these objects stun nearby towers. In Trickster God, they reportedly have 150 HP, reduced speed, and longer stun.
