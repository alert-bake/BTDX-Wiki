# Robo Bloon

<badge>Research draft</badge>

## Overview

Robo Bloons have the Lead property and fire piercing lasers that stun towers. When popped, they release a Splitting Lead Bloon of the same colour. Higher-colour variants are faster, have longer range, and stun for longer.

## Classification

- **Category:** Special Bloon / special form
- **Platform/version checked:** None; community source only.
- **HP / hits:** Red 12 HP; Blue 24; Green 36; Yellow 48; Pink 60; Rainbow 120 (reported)
- **Children / result:** Splitting Lead Bloon of matching colour
