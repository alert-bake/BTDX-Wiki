# Color Splash

## Overview

Color Splash Bloons are a mission-specific Bloon form reported to appear in the **Color Splash** mission. The community reference describes them as popping into ten Bloons of their counterpart type and moving faster than ordinary Splitting Bloons.

These are not a general property available to every Bloon in every mode.

## Reported behaviour

| Form | Reported RBE | Reported leak damage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Red | 1 | 10 lives |
| Blue | 2 | 20 lives |
| Green | 3 | 30 lives |
| Yellow | 4 | 40 lives |
| Pink | 5 | 50 lives |

The community reference says each Color Splash Bloon pops into ten Bloons of its counterpart type. The table's leak-damage values are unusual and must remain explicitly attributed to that source until reproduced in-game.

## Distinguishing it from Splitting

| Feature | Splitting | Color Splash |
|---|---|---|
| Reported children | 3 counterpart Bloons | 10 counterpart Bloons |
| Speed | Faster than ordinary counterpart | Faster than ordinary Splitting form |
| Availability | Property/form | Color Splash mission |

Do not apply Color Splash's ten-child rule to ordinary Splitting Bloons.

## Implementation status

The current source research has not yet identified a definitive Color Splash branch, exact spawn routine, or leak handler. Its gameplay description is therefore unverified report rather than source-confirmed.

The source also mentions Red Bomb Bloons in connection with a Trojan Blimp. Those are separate spawned enemies, not Color Splash forms.

## Trivia

Ten children per pop is a very different problem from the three-child Splitting property. Color Splash is less "a little extra splitting" and more "the Bloon has invited the entire extended family."
