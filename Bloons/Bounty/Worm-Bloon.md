# Worm Bloon

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

Zigzags rapidly across the screen and has a substantial health pool.

- **Associated map:** Rubber Rug
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; community documentation only.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — Worm Bloon: one Worm Bloon.

### Stage 2 — Mega Worm: higher HP, faster movement, and a stun effect on tower contact.

### Stage 3 — Iron Rubber Worm: appears instead of the ordinary Worm Bloon. It gains the Lead property and moves faster, but has less HP.

### Stage 4 — Titanium Terror: follows an Iron Rubber Worm and gains the H.T.A. property.

## Community-suggested counters

Suggested towers: HeliS, TreeM, BoomerL.

These are community strategy suggestions, not guaranteed solutions or the only viable towers.
