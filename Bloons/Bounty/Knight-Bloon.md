# Knight Bloon

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

Moves slowly, has very high HP, and uses surrounding shields that block projectiles. It also sends Sword Bloons toward the exit.

- **Associated map:** Bloon Light Throwback
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; community documentation only.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — Knight Bloon: approximately 100,000 HP according to the source. Sends Sword Bloons toward the exit, each reported as 30 RBE, and can surround itself with Shield Bloons that behave like projectile-blocking rocks.

### Stage 2 — White Knight: spawns Sword Bloons twice as quickly, with increased damage and HP, and doubles the number and HP of Shield Bloons.

### Stage 3 — Paladin Bloon: gains more HP, faster anti-projectile shields, stronger Sword Bloons, and stronger Shield Bloons. It also spawns a Giant Baby Bloon associated with Monster Mom.

### Stage 4 — Birthday Delivery: listed by the source, but no mechanics are supplied.
