# Mighty MOAB

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

The Mighty MOAB is described as the slowest and toughest Bounty Bloon, with 40,000 HP at its first stage. It periodically launches Mini M.O.A.B.s.

- **Associated map:** Monkey Fort
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; this entry is based on community documentation.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — Mighty MOAB: one Mighty MOAB with 40,000 HP.

### Stage 2 — Mighty MOAB MK II: 60,000 HP and launches M.O.A.B.s and Shielded Mini M.O.A.B.s alongside ordinary Mini M.O.A.B.s.

### Stage 3 — Mighty FIST: 80,000 HP and gains a slowly moving fist projectile that stuns towers on contact.

### Stage 4 — Mightiest MOAB: a red-tinted 160,000-HP form that also launches Super Shielded Mini M.O.A.B.s.

## Community-suggested counters

Suggested towers: TreeM, HeliM, DartlingL, DartlingS.
