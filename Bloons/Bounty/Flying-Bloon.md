# Flying Bloon

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

The Flying Bloon circles the central cloud and fires gusts that affect Bloon movement. It releases airborne allies when popped.

- **Associated map:** Sky Battles
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; this entry is based on community documentation.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — Flying Bloon: circles the middle cloud and fires gust projectiles that accelerate Bloons they touch. When popped, it releases Phoenix and Angel Bloons; each is reported as 30 RBE.

### Stage 2 — Higher Bloon: increases the health of the boss and allies, spawns allies more quickly, and makes gusts push Bloons farther.

### Stage 3 — Ascended Bloon: follows a Higher Bloon. It has more HP, stronger Phoenix Bloons, and more powerful gust projectiles.

### Stage 4 — Almighty Bloon: has more HP and creates a rain of gusts that push Bloons forward and block projectiles. It spawns after a Higher Bloon.
