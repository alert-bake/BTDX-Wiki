# Bounty Bloons

Bounty Bloons are a major BTDX feature introduced in version 1.2. They are unusually powerful encounters with multiple escalating stages. They are not ordinary round Bloons, and their mechanics are different enough to warrant a dedicated section.

## How Bounty progression works

The BTDX community wiki reports that defeating one stage unlocks the next stage of that Bounty. Most Bounty families have four stages. Big Bully, Clown, and Annoying have five stages.

The listed difficulty ranks are:

1. Low
2. Medium
3. Moderately High
4. High
5. Very High
6. Impoppable
7. Almighty

The community wiki says a Bounty level starts automatically. The exact unlock sequence, availability rules, and any build-specific exceptions need further verification.

## Reported reward table

The community source reports the following rewards per completed Bounty level:

| Rank | Monkey Money | Bloon Points | XP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 200 | 2 | 100,000 |
| Medium | 300 | 3 | 150,000 |
| Moderately High | 400 | 4 | 200,000 |
| High | 500 | 5 | 250,000 |
| Very High | 600 | 6 | 300,000 |
| Impoppable | 700 | 7 | 350,000 |
| Almighty | 900 | 9 | 450,000 |

These values are transcribed from the community wiki and have not been independently tested in Android 1.5.2 or Windows 1.5.1.

## How individual Bounty pages are structured

Each Bounty article aims to document:

- Identity, appearance, and associated map.
- How the encounter begins and progresses.
- Every known stage and its named form.
- Health, speed, leak damage, and reward details where reported.
- Attacks, spawned Bloons, hazards, and tower interactions.
- Community-suggested counters, clearly labelled as suggestions.
- Bugs, version differences, and unresolved questions.
