# Super Bloon

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

The Super Bloon crosses the screen erratically and fires projectiles resembling Super Monkey attacks. Its projectiles stun towers, and it removes lives each time it passes the screen.

- **Associated map:** Swamp Spirals
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; this entry is based on community documentation.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — Super Bloon: one 5,000-HP Bloon that fires stunning darts forward.

### Stage 2 — Super Duper Bloon: fires two lasers that reportedly double stun time. The source lists 6,500 HP with uncertainty and says the leak penalty becomes four lives.

### Stage 3 — Super Villain Bloon: has 9,000 HP and fires plasma balls with longer stun.

### Stage 4 — Super Bloon 2: has 12,500 HP and uses a Ray of Doom-like stunning beam. It removes more lives the longer it remains on-screen. After it is popped, another Bloon follows the track and uses a similar beam; its reported 4,000 HP and leak penalty are uncertain.

## Community-suggested counters
