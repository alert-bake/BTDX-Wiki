# UFO Bloon

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

UFOs move erratically across the screen at variable speed. While active, they steal money at an increasing rate. The community source says that if money reaches zero, the UFO begins draining lives instead; its wording for the life-drain rate is unclear.

- **Associated map:** Bloon Oasis
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; this entry is based on community documentation.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — UFO Bloon: one green UFO with 2,000 HP.

### Stage 2 — UFO Squad: five UFOs; one has a blue aura, 2,250 HP, and absorbs projectiles.

### Stage 3 — UFO Cluster: eight UFOs spawn in a flower formation. Four have blue auras and four are yellow. Each has 1,500 HP and reportedly drains money more slowly than a normal UFO, but the group can drain faster because several are present.

### Stage 4 — UFO Mothership: begins with the Cluster formation, then spawns a large pink 20,000-HP mothership. It drains lives and creates 1,000-HP pink UFOs every 13.33 seconds; these smaller UFOs slowly drain money and lives until the mothership is defeated.
