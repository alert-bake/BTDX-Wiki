# Clown Bloon

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

The Clown Bloon moves horizontally across the track and uses random abilities to disrupt the defence. Each ability reportedly makes it move slightly faster and increases its ability frequency.

- **Associated map:** Omega River
- **Stage count:** 5
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; this entry is based on community documentation.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — Clown Bloon: 5,000 HP. It can spawn six poppable 40-HP Star Balls that block projectiles, or teleport Bloons forward by 10% of their remaining path. Initial ability interval is about 12.63 seconds.

### Stage 2 — Joker Bloon: an 8,000-HP Joker joins a 2,500-HP Clown. The Joker can spawn six 70-HP bombs that remove 10 lives if not popped within 13 seconds, and temporarily redirect Bloons onto a random Beginner or Intermediate map path. Star Balls rise to eight and 60 HP. Initial cooldown is about 11.16 seconds.

### Stage 3 — Demon Clown: replaces the Joker with a 12,300-HP form and adds another random ability. Path redirection can select any map. The Clown has 4,000 HP.

### Stage 4 — Rubber Terror: a faster, bulkier Demon Clown with 15,300 HP. Star Balls become 80-HP Demon Balls that stun nearby towers; bombs have 90 HP. Initial cooldown is about 10.62 seconds.

### Stage 5 — Trickster God: follows two normal 3,000-HP Clowns. It has 27,300 HP and adds Hungry Hungry Bloons (999 HP) and Monkey Terrors (400 HP), both of which stun towers and block most projectiles. Demon Balls reach 150 HP; bombs reach 200 HP and stun on contact. Initial cooldown is about 17.14 seconds.
