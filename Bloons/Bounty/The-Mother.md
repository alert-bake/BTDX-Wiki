# The Mother

<badge>Research draft · Bounty Bloon</badge>

## Overview

A MOAB-like enemy that flies around the map and spawns alien Bloons. The source says it periodically stuns all towers.

- **Associated map:** Minecarts
- **Stage count:** 4
- **Bounty mode introduced:** Version 1.2, according to the community update history.
- **Platform/version checked:** None; community documentation only.

## Stage progression

### Stage 1 — The Mother: one Mother Bloon with approximately 12,000 HP. It spawns alien Bloons that remove 10 lives if they leak.

### Stage 2 — The Queen: grows larger and gains more HP. It spawns Cyclops Bloons near its position; they slowly gravitate toward the exit.

### Stage 3 — Monster Mom: returns to its original size, becomes darker, and rearranges the player's towers randomly about every two minutes. It also creates a Giant Baby Bloon with infinite HP that follows the mouse and blocks projectiles until Monster Mom is popped.

### Stage 4 — Monster Queen: combines Stage 3 behaviour with the Queen form.

## Community-suggested counters

Suggested towers: TreeM, HeliM, DartlingS.

These are community strategy suggestions, not guaranteed solutions or the only viable towers.
