# Super Storm

**Category:** Gameplay goodie  
**Goodie slot:** 15  
**In-game description:** “Super Storm?” / “Shows up at 100K+ Score”  
**Unlock cost:** 1 Goodie Token

When equipped, every 100,000 score the game spawns **10 Supra Storm enemies**. The spawn script places them just off the left side of the playfield, gives them randomized vertical positions and speeds, and sends them across the screen.

The Goodie's display name includes a question mark. The source identifies the spawned object as `obj_Supra_Storm`.
