# Lit MOAB

**Category:** Gameplay goodie  
**Goodie slot:** 12  
**In-game description:** “Lit Moab gives money when hit” / “Shows up at 75K+ Score”  
**Unlock cost:** 1 Goodie Token

When equipped, a Lit MOAB is spawned at **75,000 score**. Further spawns use the script's 375,000-point multiples: 375,000, 750,000, 1,125,000, and so on.

The store says it gives money when hit. The inspected spawn script confirms its appearance thresholds, but does not specify the cash amount or the exact payout event.
