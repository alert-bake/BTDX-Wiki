# B.A.D.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alert-bake/BTDX-source/main/sprites/spr_BAD/4f027f79-61dd-44ab-b271-fa03564807ed.png" alt="B.A.D. icon" width="112" /></p>

**Category:** Gameplay goodie  
**Goodie slot:** 13  
**In-game description:** “This Blimp is pretty Bad”  
**Unlock cost:** 1 Goodie Token

When equipped, a B.A.D. is spawned at every **666,000 score**: 666,000, 1,332,000, 1,998,000, and so on.

The Goodie spawn script creates the B.A.D. at the BloonSpawn object's position and assigns large shell/layer values. This page documents the Goodie's spawn behaviour; it does not attempt to describe every property of the B.A.D. enemy itself.
