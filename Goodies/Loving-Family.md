# Loving Family

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alert-bake/BTDX-source/main/sprites/spr_Love_Bloon/f577cd7b-76af-46d7-978e-8176c9b7d4a1.png" alt="Loving Family icon" width="112" /></p>

**Category:** Gameplay goodie  
**Goodie slot:** 11  
**In-game description:** “Loving Family Gives Extra Vitality” / “Shows up at 50K+ Score”  
**Unlock cost:** 1 Goodie Token

When equipped, the game spawns **six Love Bloons** whenever the score reaches the next 50,000-point threshold: 50,000, 100,000, 150,000, and so on.

The store calls the effect “Extra Vitality.” The spawn script confirms the six-bloon wave and its score interval, but the exact vitality payout is not established by the inspected Goodie script alone.
